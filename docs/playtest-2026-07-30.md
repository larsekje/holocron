### D. Opposed checks
Note: "Opposed checks".

The roller has an `opposed` mode (`DiceRollMode`, `OpposedPanel`) but only
mock snapshots use it — nothing in the app opens one. Build the flow:

- An entry point from the Active sheet's skill list: "Opposed…" on a skill,
  then pick the opponent and their opposing skill.
- Pool: the actor's skill dice vs. Difficulty dice from the opponent's
  opposing skill/characteristic (the higher of the two = number of purple
  dice, the lower = how many become red Challenge). Common pairs: Deception vs
  Discipline, Stealth vs Perception, Coercion vs Discipline, Charm vs Cool,
  Negotiation vs Negotiation, Brawl vs Brawl.
- A builder in `utils/diceSnapshots.ts` next to `buildSkillCheckSnapshot`
  (note: B also edits this file — rebase onto whichever lands first).

Verify: fixture, Sall: Coercion vs Rodas's Discipline → pool shows Sall's
dice vs purple/red from Rodas.

### E. Encounter roster: companions, sidelined NPCs, editable slots, picking on the shared screen
Notes: "Companion NPC", "Track npcs that is not in current encounter",
"Manipulate npc slots (remove when ded)", "Select active slot on shared
screen".

What the playtest shows: Brunt, Veem Saalo and Sliprigg are Rodas's crew, but
`Participant` only knows `isPC`, so they were adversaries: listed under
"Adversaries", given NPC slots, shown red on the shared screen. Sliprigg was
deleted at the start of encounter 1 and re-added (as "Slicer", fresh stats)
for encounter 3 — there's no way to keep someone tracked but out of the fight.
And deleting the killed Pirate Crew mid-encounter left its slot behind
(slots are only disabled for *dead* participants, `utils/initiativeSlots.ts`).

1. **Companions**: an optional `side: 'PC' | 'NPC'` (default from `isPC`) on
   `Participant`, toggled from the target row's ⋮ menu. Companions list under
   the PCs (e.g. "Party"), take PC-team slots when initiative is rolled
   (`components/turnbar/InitiativeModal.tsx`), count for PC slot
   eligibility/disable logic, and show as PC team on the shared screen
   (`sync/snapshot.ts`).
2. **Sidelined**: an `offstage?: boolean` that keeps wounds/strain/pouch but
   removes the participant from initiative, slot counting, the shared screen
   and the live lists; a collapsed "Off-stage" section brings them back
   ("Bring in" adds a slot when an encounter is running).
3. **Slots**: removing a participant mid-encounter removes one trailing slot
   of their team (adjusting `currentTurnIndex`); clicking a slot in
   `InitiativeOrder` offers remove / add PC slot / add NPC slot.
4. **Shared screen picking**: with companions on the PC team, the player view's
   "Who's acting?" (`player/PlayerTurnControls.tsx`) lists them; also let
   players tap a name in `PlayerRoster` during an open PC slot.

This is the largest brief; split into one commit per numbered item.

Verify: fixture; mark Brunt/Veem/Sliprigg as companions → they move under

Sliprigg → gone from the list and slots, back with stats intact.
